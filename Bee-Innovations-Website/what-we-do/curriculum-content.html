Curriculum & Content

A programme built around three things working together: thinking, technology, and doing. Every lesson is designed to build on the one before it, so skill compounds year over year instead of resetting each September.

Our content doesn't treat STEM as a subject to memorize and test. It's a set of tools students pick up, level by level, so that what they imagine stops being a thought and starts being something they can hold, show, and improve.

Each grade's content is scoped against the level before and after it, so a skill learned once gets used again — and gets harder — the next year. That's what makes the ladder worth climbing: by the top, a student isn't holding a stack of disconnected facts, they're holding the compounding result of everything they built along the way.

Full grade-by-grade scope and the grade × skill matrix live on the Curriculum page.
